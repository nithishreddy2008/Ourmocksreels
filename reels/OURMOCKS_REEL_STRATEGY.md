## 1. What we stand for (source of every reel)

| | Wording from the platform |
|---|---|
| **Promise** | "Your dream deserves more than one path." |
| **Mission** | Help students understand entrance exams after Class 12, the opportunities they open, and the steps needed to apply. We begin with CUET UG, IISER IAT, NEST and COMEDK UGET. |
| **Vision** | A future where students choose a path with awareness and confidence, supported by clear information and purposeful preparation. |
| **Method** | Discover. Understand. Decide. |

**Positioning line for social:** *Beyond JEE & NEET. Four exams, one place to practise.*

**Our two edges (repeat in every brand reel and pinned post):**
1. **Domain-subject test series for CUET UG.** Most CUET prep sells only English and General Test. We have full series for 16 subjects, 15 tests each: Accountancy, Agriculture, Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, Economics, English, General Test, Geography, Hindi, History, Mass Media, Mathematics, Physics, Political Science and Sanskrit.
2. **Reviewed before you see it.** Every test is reviewed and validated by university professors and PhD scholars.

**Equal weight to all four exams.** In every brand piece CUET UG, IISER IAT, NISER NEST and COMEDK UGET get the same screen time. Single-exam reels rotate evenly through the week.

### Claim guardrails (protects the brand; ASCI-safe)
- **Don't say "all domain subjects" yet.** There are 7 syllabus subjects with no tests: Anthropology, Business Studies, Environmental Science, Home Science, Performing Arts, Psychology and Sociology.
  - Say **"16 CUET UG subjects, 15 full tests each"**, or **"domain subjects nobody else covers"**.
  - Switch to "every domain subject" the day the last series ships.
- **Have proof for the review claim.** Have a named panel ready (names, designation, university), even 3–4 people. The first critic who asks "which professors?" should get a link, not silence. A reviewer-intro reel is planned in Phase 2.
- **"Free Test 1" only for exams where it is live.** Today that is CUET UG; IAT, NEST and COMEDK open as each paper is released.
- **Real campus photos only, always credited.** The photos are Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Credit goes in the caption's last line or the first comment.
- Never use IISER, NISER, NTA or COMEDK logos. Keep "not affiliated" in the bio.

### Visual styles (rotate so the grid never looks templated)
1. **Kinetic type over real campus photos.** Ken Burns pans on real photos, bold serif and italic. Brand intros.
2. **Animated chat** (WhatsApp/DM style). Bubbles pop in with typing dots. Student↔student and parent↔parent.
3. **Split-screen dinner-table skit.** Parent on the left, child on the right, illustrated or presenter cut-outs, subtitles. Parent↔child.
4. **Data-viz explainer.** Chapter-weightage bars, PYQ heatmaps. Built from our own analysis PDFs; the funnel reels.
5. **Presenter talking head.** The academy presenter, or a real team member, with burned-in captions. Trust reels.
6. **Opuscar film styles.** Whiteboard sketch, Swiss motion graphics, or chalkboard for "explainer" days.

**Voice on reels:** Indian English subtitles by default, because most people watch muted. Add a TTS or real voice on conversation reels.

## 6. Algorithm playbook (what moves reach in 2026)

- **Sends per reach** (DM shares) and **watch-through** are the strongest signals. Every reel needs a reason to send it: "send this to the friend still saying JEE or nothing".
- **First 2 seconds decide distribution.** Text on screen at frame 1, motion from frame 1, no logo intro.
- **Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers first): use for every Phase 1 reel to test hooks without denting the grid.
- **Search is caption text.** Put the phrases people type ("CUET domain subjects mock test", "IISER IAT preparation") as plain words in the caption. Use **≤5 hashtags** as labels; Instagram capped hashtags at 5 in Dec 2025.
- **Comment keywords** lift comments, which is good, but Instagram demotes obvious "engagement bait". Make every keyword reel deliver real value inside the video too, so it isn't just a gate.
- **Posting windows (IST) to test:**
  - Students: 1–2 pm and 7–9:30 pm.
  - Parents: 8:30–10 pm.
  - Run each Phase 1 reel at a different slot, then keep the winner.
- **Length:** brand reels 20–35 s, funnel reels 15–25 s, skits 30–45 s.
- **Cover frames** carry a 3–4 word title, so the grid reads like a menu.
