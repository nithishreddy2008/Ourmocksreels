There are four more doors. Most of us hear about them when it's already too late.

We built OurMocks so you can practise for all four in one place. Even your domain subjects. CUET UG mock tests in 16 subjects, Accountancy to Sanskrit, 15 full tests each.

Who checks them? University professors and PhD scholars, before you ever see a question.
